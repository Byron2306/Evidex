# Brand + Naming (Recommendation)

## Recommendation (practical)
- Don’t keep it anonymous once you sell beyond 1–3 “friends & family” clients.
- For corporates, trust signals matter: a name, a 1‑page website/one‑pager, clear scope, and a confidentiality statement.
- You *can* stay lightweight: brand the **product** first, and keep the operating entity simple.

## Chosen name
**EVIDEX**
- Short for “evidence index”.
- Works for audit, VAT evidence, ESG/CSI, and grant reporting.

## Alternative names (optional)
- EvidencePack
- AuditTrail Pack
- EvidenceVault
- TracePack
- VerifiPack

## When to stay “quiet”
- Early validation: first 1–3 clients via warm intros.
- Consultancy channel: offer white‑label delivery (their brand, your engine).

## When to be public
- Any cold outreach (LinkedIn/email), any paid ads, any procurement/PO work.
- If you want referrals: a name + URL increases conversion massively.
