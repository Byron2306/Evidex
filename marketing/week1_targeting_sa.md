# Week 1 (South Africa) — Targeting & Setup

## Campaign A — Audit Readiness (LinkedIn)
**Objective:** Lead gen / website visits

**Job titles (start narrow):**
- Chief Financial Officer, Finance Director
- Head of Internal Audit, Internal Audit Manager
- Risk Manager, Head of Risk, Compliance Manager
- Financial Controller, Finance Manager

**Industries (start with these):**
- Financial Services, Insurance
- Retail, FMCG
- Telecoms
- Manufacturing
- Mining (if you can tailor language to heavy compliance)

**Company size:** 200+ employees (or 500+) 

**Message:** sampling-ready evidence pack, QA report, conservative claims, 48h turnaround

## Campaign B — Tax/VAT Evidence (Google Search + LinkedIn)
**Objective:** High intent leads

**Search keywords (phrase/exact to start):**
- "VAT audit support"
- "VAT evidence pack"
- "SARS VAT audit" (be careful with policy; don’t imply affiliation)
- "audit file preparation"
- "audit evidence table"

**Negative keywords:** jobs, salary, training, course, free template, pdf download

**Message:** reconcile claims to source docs; review-ready structure; not legal/tax advice

## Campaign C — Consultancy Partner (LinkedIn)
**Objective:** Partner acquisition

**Targets:**
- Grant compliance consultants
- Audit support / risk advisory firms
- ESG/CSI consultancies
- Accounting practices offering compliance services

**Offer:** white-label delivery, batch (5/10) pricing, you stay client-facing

## Landing page structure (simple)
- Hero: “Audit-ready evidence pack in 48 hours”
- 3 bullets: Evidence table, Sources folder, Quality report
- Proof: show the ZIP folder layout screenshot (no client data)
- CTA: “Send a test job” / “Book 10-min fit check”
- Compliance line: “Not legal/tax advice”
